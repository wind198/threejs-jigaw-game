# Board-first responsive playroom
The board stays in the original six-unit coordinate system with a thinner 6.3-unit frame. Camera framing prioritizes board size, reserves enough space for the largest piece footprint, and permits overlap. All remaining pieces are present; there is no pagination. Landscape uses side trays and portrait uses a bottom scatter area. Seeded jittered grids maximize minimum spacing within available bounds, with deterministic vertical stacking and nearest-hit raycasting. Fully concealed pieces may occur in very compact viewports; Shuffle redistributes all remaining pieces. A picked piece rises above the maximum stack. Failed placements return to their original home. Successful placements do not move other pieces. Resize and shuffle wait for idle states, leaving locked pieces untouched.
The compact header and single toolbar leave most of the screen to the canvas. The hint is a dismissible overlay rather than resizing the scene. No persistent footer. No new dependencies. QA: 4/9/25 pieces, 320x568 and 390x844, landscape, shuffle while selected, overlapping hit targets, orientation changes, hint, cancellation and retry. Browser measurements are pending.
