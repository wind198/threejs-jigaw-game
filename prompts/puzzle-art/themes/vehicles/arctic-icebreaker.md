# Scene

An icebreaker ship carving a path through blue ice with penguins watching from floes.

# Composition

Ship center cutting ice; ice floes left and right; penguins landmarks; distant aurora; wake channel.

# Color direction

Hull red-white, ice cyan, aurora mint, ocean deep blue channel.

# Special instructions

All subjects should be reassuring and friendly for ages 3–10. Create one unified scene with visible clues in every quadrant, not a collage or a set of stickers. Prefer rich midtones that separate clearly from warm cream and beige board colors.
