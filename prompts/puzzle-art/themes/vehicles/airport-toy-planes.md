# Scene

Colorful small planes on a playful runway with hangars and windsocks.

# Composition

Two planes mid; runway stripes; hangar left; windsock right; control tower simple shape (no text).

# Color direction

Planes yellow and teal, runway grey-blue, hangar coral, grass green edges.

# Special instructions

All subjects should be reassuring and friendly for ages 3–10. Create one unified scene with visible clues in every quadrant, not a collage or a set of stickers. Prefer rich midtones that separate clearly from warm cream and beige board colors.
