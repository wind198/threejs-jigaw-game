# Scene

An airboat speeding through a mangrove river with birds, roots and mist.

# Composition

Airboat mid; fan cage clear circle; mangrove arches; birds upper; mist bands; water ripples.

# Color direction

Airboat lime-teal, mangrove umber-green, water brown-teal clear, bird scarlet.

# Special instructions

All subjects should be reassuring and friendly for ages 3–10. Create one unified scene with visible clues in every quadrant, not a collage or a set of stickers. Prefer rich midtones that separate clearly from warm cream and beige board colors.
