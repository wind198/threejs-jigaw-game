# Scene

Multiple shuttles and service trucks at a colorful spaceport under twin moons.

# Composition

Shuttles of different colors as landmarks; fuel trucks; hangar arches; twin moons upper; runway lights.

# Color direction

Shuttle teal coral gold, tarmac cool grey, moons lavender, sky indigo-dusk.

# Special instructions

All subjects should be reassuring and friendly for ages 3–10. Create one unified scene with visible clues in every quadrant, not a collage or a set of stickers. Prefer rich midtones that separate clearly from warm cream and beige board colors.
