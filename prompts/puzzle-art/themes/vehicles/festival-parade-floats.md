# Scene

A joyful parade of colorful floats with flowers, animals and musicians (original designs, no logos/text).

# Composition

Three floats across mid as distinct landmarks; crowd color blocks lower; banners without text; confetti dots upper.

# Color direction

Floats magenta teal gold, flower masses, street warm grey, confetti multicolor but soft.

# Special instructions

All subjects should be reassuring and friendly for ages 3–10. Create one unified scene with visible clues in every quadrant, not a collage or a set of stickers. Prefer rich midtones that separate clearly from warm cream and beige board colors.
