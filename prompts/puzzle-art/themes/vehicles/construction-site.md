# Scene

Friendly toy-like excavator, dump truck and crane safely building a small playful house.

# Composition

Vehicles each distinct by silhouette and position; orderly construction materials, cones, worker-free scene and neat site.

# Color direction

Yellow machines with teal, brick-red and navy structural accents. Keep yellow bodies distinct from sand/beige ground.

# Special instructions

All subjects should be reassuring and friendly for ages 3–10. Create one unified scene with visible clues in every quadrant, not a collage or a set of stickers.
