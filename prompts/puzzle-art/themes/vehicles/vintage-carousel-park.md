# Scene

A detailed carousel with ornate horses, canopy lights and a park around it (no readable text).

# Composition

Carousel center circle; horses as repeating-but-varied landmarks; canopy roof; park trees and benches around.

# Color direction

Canopy teal-gold, horses coral and white, lights warm amber, park greens.

# Special instructions

All subjects should be reassuring and friendly for ages 3–10. Create one unified scene with visible clues in every quadrant, not a collage or a set of stickers. Prefer rich midtones that separate clearly from warm cream and beige board colors.
