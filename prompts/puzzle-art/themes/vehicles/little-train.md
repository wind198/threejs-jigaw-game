# Scene

A cheerful toy-like steam train travelling through rolling hills past a station and friendly farm animals.

# Composition

Large engine on lower third, two differently colored carriages, small station to the right, trees, bridge and cloud landmarks.

# Color direction

Bright blue engine, coral carriage, gold trim and rich green hills separated against pale sky.

# Special instructions

All subjects should be reassuring and friendly for ages 3–10. Create one unified scene with visible clues in every quadrant, not a collage or a set of stickers.
