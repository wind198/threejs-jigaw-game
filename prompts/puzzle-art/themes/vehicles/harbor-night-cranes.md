# Scene

A cozy harbor at dusk with cranes, cargo ships and glowing warehouses (friendly, not industrial grim).

# Composition

Cranes as tall landmarks; ships mid-water; warehouse lights; reflections; lighthouse far right.

# Color direction

Dusk violet-coral sky, crane teal silhouettes with lights, water mirror gold, warehouse warm windows.

# Special instructions

All subjects should be reassuring and friendly for ages 3–10. Create one unified scene with visible clues in every quadrant, not a collage or a set of stickers. Prefer rich midtones that separate clearly from warm cream and beige board colors.
