# Scene

A turquoise reef scene with a clownfish, a sea turtle and colorful coral towers.

# Composition

Turtle mid-left swimming; clownfish lower-right among anemones; coral pillars upper and mid as vertical landmarks; sandy floor bottom strip.

# Color direction

Teal and sapphire water, coral pinks and oranges, turtle olive shell, bright clownfish stripes.

# Special instructions

All subjects should be reassuring and friendly for ages 3–10. Create one unified scene with visible clues in every quadrant, not a collage or a set of stickers. Prefer rich midtones that separate clearly from warm cream and beige board colors.
