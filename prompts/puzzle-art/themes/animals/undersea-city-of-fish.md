# Scene

A rich underwater vista with schools of fish, a whale shark silhouette, coral arches and kelp forests.

# Composition

Whale shark mid-background; coral arches foreground left and right; fish schools as curved bands; treasure chest detail lower-center; light shafts from above.

# Color direction

Deep sapphire water, coral oranges, fish yellows and teals, soft gold light shafts.

# Special instructions

All subjects should be reassuring and friendly for ages 3–10. Create one unified scene with visible clues in every quadrant, not a collage or a set of stickers. Prefer rich midtones that separate clearly from warm cream and beige board colors.
