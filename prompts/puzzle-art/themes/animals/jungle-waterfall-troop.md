# Scene

A lively rainforest with monkeys on vines, a macaw in flight, butterflies and a cascading waterfall.

# Composition

Waterfall vertical center-right; monkeys on vines left and upper; macaw upper-right arc; layered canopy, rocks and flowers filling every quadrant with clear landmarks.

# Color direction

Layered greens from jade to pine, macaw scarlet and cobalt, waterfall cyan, orchid pink accents.

# Special instructions

All subjects should be reassuring and friendly for ages 3–10. Create one unified scene with visible clues in every quadrant, not a collage or a set of stickers. Prefer rich midtones that separate clearly from warm cream and beige board colors.
