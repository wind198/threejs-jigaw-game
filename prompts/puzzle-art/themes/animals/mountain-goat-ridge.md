# Scene

Sure-footed goats on a rocky alpine ridge with wildflowers, eagles and distant snow peaks.

# Composition

Primary goat on center rock; second goat left ledge; eagle upper-right; flower patches lower; layered peaks and clouds across depth.

# Color direction

Stone greys with teal lichen, white goat coats, alpine flower magenta, sky ultramarine.

# Special instructions

All subjects should be reassuring and friendly for ages 3–10. Create one unified scene with visible clues in every quadrant, not a collage or a set of stickers. Prefer rich midtones that separate clearly from warm cream and beige board colors.
