# Scene

Colorful songbirds among blossoming orchard trees with ladders, baskets and a nesting box.

# Composition

Blooming trees filling upper half; three bird species on different branches; ladder mid-left; basket lower-right; nesting box mid-right.

# Color direction

Blossom pink-white, bird blues and yellows, ladder warm wood, leaf spring green.

# Special instructions

All subjects should be reassuring and friendly for ages 3–10. Create one unified scene with visible clues in every quadrant, not a collage or a set of stickers. Prefer rich midtones that separate clearly from warm cream and beige board colors.
