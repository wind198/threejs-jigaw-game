# Scene

Three cheerful penguins waddling across blue ice under a sunny polar sky.

# Composition

Penguins in a gentle arc across the middle; ice shelf foreground with clear ridges; distant iceberg left; soft sun disc upper-right.

# Color direction

Inky black and crisp white penguin bodies, saturated ice blues, peach-gold sunlight — no grey mush.

# Special instructions

All subjects should be reassuring and friendly for ages 3–10. Create one unified scene with visible clues in every quadrant, not a collage or a set of stickers. Prefer rich midtones that separate clearly from warm cream and beige board colors.
