# Scene

A white arctic fox curled near blue ice crystals with northern lights soft in the sky.

# Composition

Fox center-right; crystalline ice mounds left; aurora bands across upper third; footprints in snow lower edge.

# Color direction

Soft white fox with blue shadow edges, mint-to-violet aurora, ice cyan — avoid flat cream snow.

# Special instructions

All subjects should be reassuring and friendly for ages 3–10. Create one unified scene with visible clues in every quadrant, not a collage or a set of stickers. Prefer rich midtones that separate clearly from warm cream and beige board colors.
