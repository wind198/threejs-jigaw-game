# Scene

Several large butterflies of different colors dancing above wildflowers with a bee nearby.

# Composition

Three butterflies spaced across upper half as landmarks; wildflower clumps in each lower quadrant; bee near center flower.

# Color direction

Butterfly wings in coral, sapphire and gold; flower magenta and yellow; deep green stems.

# Special instructions

All subjects should be reassuring and friendly for ages 3–10. Create one unified scene with visible clues in every quadrant, not a collage or a set of stickers. Prefer rich midtones that separate clearly from warm cream and beige board colors.
