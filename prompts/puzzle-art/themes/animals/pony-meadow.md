# Scene

A gentle brown pony standing in a flower meadow under a big blue sky.

# Composition

Pony full-body center; daisy clusters foreground; fence posts mid-right; rolling green hill background.

# Color direction

Warm chestnut coat, saturated meadow greens, white-yellow daisies, medium blue sky.

# Special instructions

All subjects should be reassuring and friendly for ages 3–10. Create one unified scene with visible clues in every quadrant, not a collage or a set of stickers. Prefer rich midtones that separate clearly from warm cream and beige board colors.
