# Scene

A smiling giraffe stretching its neck to nibble leaves from a single tall acacia.

# Composition

Giraffe dominates left-center; acacia crown upper-right; grassy savanna lower band with two distinct bushes; blue sky with one cloud.

# Color direction

Honey-gold giraffe spots, deep olive canopy, vivid grass green, clear sky blue.

# Special instructions

All subjects should be reassuring and friendly for ages 3–10. Create one unified scene with visible clues in every quadrant, not a collage or a set of stickers. Prefer rich midtones that separate clearly from warm cream and beige board colors.
