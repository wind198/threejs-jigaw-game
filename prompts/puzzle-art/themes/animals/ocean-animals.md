# Scene

A friendly sea turtle swimming with a colorful fish and a small smiling octopus over a coral reef.

# Composition

Turtle upper center, octopus lower right, coral formations and shells at lower corners, sunbeams through blue water.

# Color direction

Vary turquoise, royal blue and coral red. Avoid one large flat blue region; add light-to-dark sea depth.

# Special instructions

All subjects should be reassuring and friendly for ages 3–10. Create one unified scene with visible clues in every quadrant, not a collage or a set of stickers.
