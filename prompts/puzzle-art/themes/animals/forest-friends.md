# Scene

A friendly fox and a white rabbit playing in a woodland clearing.

# Composition

Fox and rabbit central and fully visible; foreground colorful mushrooms and flowers, middle-ground tree stump, background distinct blue-green trees and gentle hills.

# Color direction

Warm fox coral, clean blue sky, deeper pine green trees and golden flowers; avoid beige grass and cream-only sky.

# Special instructions

All subjects should be reassuring and friendly for ages 3–10. Create one unified scene with visible clues in every quadrant, not a collage or a set of stickers.
