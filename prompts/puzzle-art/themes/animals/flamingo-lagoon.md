# Scene

Two pink flamingos standing in shallow turquoise water with reflections and palm tips.

# Composition

Flamingos center mirrored in water; palm fronds upper corners; distant dunes left; ripple patterns as lower texture.

# Color direction

Vivid flamingo pink, lagoon teal, palm emerald, sand gold.

# Special instructions

All subjects should be reassuring and friendly for ages 3–10. Create one unified scene with visible clues in every quadrant, not a collage or a set of stickers. Prefer rich midtones that separate clearly from warm cream and beige board colors.
