# Scene

A fennec fox under a starry desert sky with cacti, dunes and glowing lantern beetles.

# Composition

Fennec center dunes; tall cacti left and right landmarks; beetle lights scattered; dune ridges creating Z-depth; Milky Way soft band above.

# Color direction

Sand golds and rose dunes, indigo night sky, cactus teal, warm beetle glows — friendly night.

# Special instructions

All subjects should be reassuring and friendly for ages 3–10. Create one unified scene with visible clues in every quadrant, not a collage or a set of stickers. Prefer rich midtones that separate clearly from warm cream and beige board colors.
