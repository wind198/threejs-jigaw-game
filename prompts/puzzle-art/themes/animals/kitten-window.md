# Scene

A fluffy orange kitten perched on a sunny windowsill looking at butterflies outside.

# Composition

Kitten fills the center; bright window frame as a clear rectangle landmark; three oversized butterflies upper-right; potted plant lower-left.

# Color direction

Warm tangerine fur, cobalt sky beyond the glass, teal plant leaves, lilac butterflies.

# Special instructions

All subjects should be reassuring and friendly for ages 3–10. Create one unified scene with visible clues in every quadrant, not a collage or a set of stickers. Prefer rich midtones that separate clearly from warm cream and beige board colors.
