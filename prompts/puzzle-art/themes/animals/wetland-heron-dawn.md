# Scene

A tall heron in misty wetlands among reeds, frogs, dragonflies and a wooden walkway.

# Composition

Heron tall center; walkway diagonal lower-left; reed clusters as vertical rhythm; frogs and dragonflies as small readable landmarks; dawn sky gradient.

# Color direction

Heron slate blue, dawn coral-to-lavender sky, reed gold-green, water mirror teal.

# Special instructions

All subjects should be reassuring and friendly for ages 3–10. Create one unified scene with visible clues in every quadrant, not a collage or a set of stickers. Prefer rich midtones that separate clearly from warm cream and beige board colors.
