# Scene

A yellow duckling floating on a round pond with lily pads and a friendly frog.

# Composition

Duckling center; circular pond as clear shape; two lily pads left; frog on a pad right; reeds as vertical bars at the edges.

# Color direction

Bright lemon yellow duckling, teal water, lime lily pads, coral frog — strong subject separation.

# Special instructions

All subjects should be reassuring and friendly for ages 3–10. Create one unified scene with visible clues in every quadrant, not a collage or a set of stickers. Prefer rich midtones that separate clearly from warm cream and beige board colors.
