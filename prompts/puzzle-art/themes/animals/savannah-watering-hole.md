# Scene

Giraffes, zebras and a lion family sharing a watering hole at golden hour with birds overhead.

# Composition

Watering hole oval center-low; giraffes left, zebras right, lions foreground; birds upper arc; acacias and sun on horizon.

# Color direction

Golden-hour oranges, zebra high-contrast stripes, giraffe ochre, water teal reflections.

# Special instructions

All subjects should be reassuring and friendly for ages 3–10. Create one unified scene with visible clues in every quadrant, not a collage or a set of stickers. Prefer rich midtones that separate clearly from warm cream and beige board colors.
