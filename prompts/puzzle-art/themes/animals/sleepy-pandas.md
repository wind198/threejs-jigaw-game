# Scene

Two round pandas lounging on a mossy rock beside a calm bamboo stream.

# Composition

Pandas large and central; bamboo stalks left and right as vertical landmarks; smooth blue-green water in the lower third; soft distant hills.

# Color direction

Deep charcoal panda patches, fresh jade bamboo, turquoise water, soft coral flowers — avoid pale beige ground.

# Special instructions

All subjects should be reassuring and friendly for ages 3–10. Create one unified scene with visible clues in every quadrant, not a collage or a set of stickers. Prefer rich midtones that separate clearly from warm cream and beige board colors.
