# Scene

Multiple pandas playing among dense bamboo with a stone bridge, stream and lanterns.

# Composition

Bridge mid-horizontal; pandas in three spots (bridge, bank, climbing); stream S-curve; lanterns as warm dots; bamboo density with clear openings.

# Color direction

Jade bamboo, charcoal-white pandas, warm lantern amber, stream turquoise.

# Special instructions

All subjects should be reassuring and friendly for ages 3–10. Create one unified scene with visible clues in every quadrant, not a collage or a set of stickers. Prefer rich midtones that separate clearly from warm cream and beige board colors.
