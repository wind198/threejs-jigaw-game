# Scene

A soft white rabbit sitting among oversized orange carrots in a garden patch.

# Composition

Rabbit central; three giant carrots radiating as landmarks; leafy greens lower edge; wooden garden stake right.

# Color direction

Clean white fur edged by rich soil brown, carrot orange, leafy emerald, soft lavender sky.

# Special instructions

All subjects should be reassuring and friendly for ages 3–10. Create one unified scene with visible clues in every quadrant, not a collage or a set of stickers. Prefer rich midtones that separate clearly from warm cream and beige board colors.
