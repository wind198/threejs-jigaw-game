# Scene

An elephant family walking past acacia silhouettes as the sun rises over the savanna.

# Composition

Adult elephant center with calf beside; two acacias left and right; sun disc low on horizon; foreground dusty path with distinct stones.

# Color direction

Warm coral sunrise, deep purple-brown elephants, gold grass, teal sky upper band.

# Special instructions

All subjects should be reassuring and friendly for ages 3–10. Create one unified scene with visible clues in every quadrant, not a collage or a set of stickers. Prefer rich midtones that separate clearly from warm cream and beige board colors.
