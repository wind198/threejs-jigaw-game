# Scene

A cheerful red barn with a cow, a sheep and two chickens on a sunny farm.

# Composition

Barn anchors left, large cow and sheep center-right, fence and straw bale foreground, distant green hills and clouds.

# Color direction

Distinct barn red/coral, saturated grassy green, medium blue sky and yellow hay. Keep white sheep outlined by darker greenery.

# Special instructions

All subjects should be reassuring and friendly for ages 3–10. Create one unified scene with visible clues in every quadrant, not a collage or a set of stickers.
