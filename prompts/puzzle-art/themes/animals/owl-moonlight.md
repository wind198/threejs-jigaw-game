# Scene

A round friendly owl perched on a thick tree branch with a soft glowing moon behind.

# Composition

Owl large and central on a horizontal branch; moon disc upper-left; two hanging leaves lower-right; night-blue sky with sparse stars.

# Color direction

Amber owl eyes and feathers, deep indigo sky, cream moon, teal leaves — cozy not spooky.

# Special instructions

All subjects should be reassuring and friendly for ages 3–10. Create one unified scene with visible clues in every quadrant, not a collage or a set of stickers. Prefer rich midtones that separate clearly from warm cream and beige board colors.
