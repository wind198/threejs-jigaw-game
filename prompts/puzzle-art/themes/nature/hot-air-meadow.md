# Scene

A peaceful meadow with two colorful hot-air balloons drifting above rolling hills.

# Composition

Balloons upper-left and upper-right; meadow flowers lower; hills mid-band; path S-curve.

# Color direction

Balloon scarlet and teal, meadow greens, hill blue-green, sky soft gold near horizon.

# Special instructions

All subjects should be reassuring and friendly for ages 3–10. Create one unified scene with visible clues in every quadrant, not a collage or a set of stickers. Prefer rich midtones that separate clearly from warm cream and beige board colors.
