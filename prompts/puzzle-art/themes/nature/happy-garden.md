# Scene

A sunny garden with a bright watering can, three large flowers and a butterfly.

# Composition

Flowers across midground, watering can near lower-left, a bee, distinct leafy plants and a wooden gate.

# Color direction

Use clear teal foliage, sunflower yellow, violet and coral petals against a sky-blue background rather than a beige wash.

# Special instructions

All subjects should be reassuring and friendly for ages 3–10. Create one unified scene with visible clues in every quadrant, not a collage or a set of stickers.
