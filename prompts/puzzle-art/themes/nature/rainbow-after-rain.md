# Scene

A bright rainbow arcing over green hills with a shiny puddle reflecting colors.

# Composition

Rainbow as a large arc landmark; hills mid-band; puddle lower-center mirror; two trees left and right.

# Color direction

Rainbow saturated bands, hill greens, puddle blues, tree teal canopies.

# Special instructions

All subjects should be reassuring and friendly for ages 3–10. Create one unified scene with visible clues in every quadrant, not a collage or a set of stickers. Prefer rich midtones that separate clearly from warm cream and beige board colors.
