# Scene

A cozy grove of red-capped mushrooms among moss and tiny flowers.

# Composition

Largest mushroom center; smaller mushrooms flanking; moss carpet lower; soft tree trunks background.

# Color direction

Mushroom scarlet with white dots, moss jade, flower yellow accents, trunk cool grey.

# Special instructions

All subjects should be reassuring and friendly for ages 3–10. Create one unified scene with visible clues in every quadrant, not a collage or a set of stickers. Prefer rich midtones that separate clearly from warm cream and beige board colors.
