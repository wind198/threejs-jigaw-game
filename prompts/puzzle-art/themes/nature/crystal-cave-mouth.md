# Scene

The glowing mouth of a crystal cave opening onto a green valley.

# Composition

Cave arch center with crystal teeth; valley vista through opening; mossy rocks foreground; soft light inside.

# Color direction

Crystal cyan and violet, cave slate, valley green, warm interior glow.

# Special instructions

All subjects should be reassuring and friendly for ages 3–10. Create one unified scene with visible clues in every quadrant, not a collage or a set of stickers. Prefer rich midtones that separate clearly from warm cream and beige board colors.
