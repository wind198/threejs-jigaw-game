# Scene

Twisting mangrove roots in teal water with herons, crabs and hanging moss.

# Composition

Root arches as interlocking landmarks; water channels; heron mid-right; crabs lower-left; canopy gaps showing sky.

# Color direction

Root umber, water teal, moss lime, heron slate blue.

# Special instructions

All subjects should be reassuring and friendly for ages 3–10. Create one unified scene with visible clues in every quadrant, not a collage or a set of stickers. Prefer rich midtones that separate clearly from warm cream and beige board colors.
