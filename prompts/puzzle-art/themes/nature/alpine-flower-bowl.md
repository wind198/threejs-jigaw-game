# Scene

A high alpine bowl filled with dense wildflowers, meltwater streams and granite peaks.

# Composition

Flower mosaic filling lower two-thirds with color groups as landmarks; streams threading; peaks upper band; stones mid.

# Color direction

Flower patches of magenta, gold and blue; granite cool grey; meltwater cyan.

# Special instructions

All subjects should be reassuring and friendly for ages 3–10. Create one unified scene with visible clues in every quadrant, not a collage or a set of stickers. Prefer rich midtones that separate clearly from warm cream and beige board colors.
