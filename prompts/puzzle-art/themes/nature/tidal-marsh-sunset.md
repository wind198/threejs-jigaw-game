# Scene

A tidal marsh at sunset with winding channels, reeds, wading birds and mirrored sky colors.

# Composition

Channel S-curves as structure; reed masses left and right; birds mid; sunset gradient sky reflected in water; boardwalk hint lower-left.

# Color direction

Sunset coral and violet, reed gold, water reflections teal-pink, bird silhouettes with color edges.

# Special instructions

All subjects should be reassuring and friendly for ages 3–10. Create one unified scene with visible clues in every quadrant, not a collage or a set of stickers. Prefer rich midtones that separate clearly from warm cream and beige board colors.
