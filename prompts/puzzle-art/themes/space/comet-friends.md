# Scene

A sparkling comet with a smiling face trailing glitter past two small planets.

# Composition

Comet diagonal from upper-left to lower-right; planets left and right; glitter trail as landmark band.

# Color direction

Comet gold-white, trail cyan sparkles, planets coral and violet, space blue.

# Special instructions

All subjects should be reassuring and friendly for ages 3–10. Create one unified scene with visible clues in every quadrant, not a collage or a set of stickers. Prefer rich midtones that separate clearly from warm cream and beige board colors.
