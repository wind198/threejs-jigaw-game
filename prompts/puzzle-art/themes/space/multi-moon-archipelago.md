# Scene

Several differently colored moons forming an archipelago of floating isles with bridges of light.

# Composition

Five moons/isles of distinct colors as landmarks; light bridges connecting; rocket path; nebula backdrop.

# Color direction

Moons in coral, teal, gold, violet, mint; bridges luminous white-gold; nebula blue-purple.

# Special instructions

All subjects should be reassuring and friendly for ages 3–10. Create one unified scene with visible clues in every quadrant, not a collage or a set of stickers. Prefer rich midtones that separate clearly from warm cream and beige board colors.
