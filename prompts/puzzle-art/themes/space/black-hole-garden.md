# Scene

A stylized gentle accretion glow around a soft dark center, with flower-like energy petals (child-friendly, not scary).

# Composition

Dark soft core center; glowing petal rings; orbiting asteroids with plants; distant stars; clear radial landmarks.

# Color direction

Glow gold-to-magenta rings, teal asteroids, plant greens, indigo outer space.

# Special instructions

All subjects should be reassuring and friendly for ages 3–10. Create one unified scene with visible clues in every quadrant, not a collage or a set of stickers. Prefer rich midtones that separate clearly from warm cream and beige board colors.
