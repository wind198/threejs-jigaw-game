# Scene

An icy moon surface with sparkling geysers shooting glittery plumes into space.

# Composition

Geyser plumes vertical landmarks; cracked ice plains; distant gas giant upper; rover tiny mid.

# Color direction

Ice cyan-white, plume glitter teal, gas giant banded peach, space deep blue.

# Special instructions

All subjects should be reassuring and friendly for ages 3–10. Create one unified scene with visible clues in every quadrant, not a collage or a set of stickers. Prefer rich midtones that separate clearly from warm cream and beige board colors.
