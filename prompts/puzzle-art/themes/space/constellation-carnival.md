# Scene

A carnival of constellation creatures (bear, swan, crab) made of connected stars above a planet fairground.

# Composition

Three constellation figures in upper half; fairground tents and wheels lower (no text); connecting star lines; planet curve.

# Color direction

Star gold figures, tent coral and teal, planet lavender night side, sky indigo.

# Special instructions

All subjects should be reassuring and friendly for ages 3–10. Create one unified scene with visible clues in every quadrant, not a collage or a set of stickers. Prefer rich midtones that separate clearly from warm cream and beige board colors.
