# Scene

A mountain observatory under a spectacular sky with telescopes, aurora and layered constellations.

# Composition

Observatory dome mid-peak; telescope tubes as clear shapes; aurora bands; constellation figures; path switchbacks lower.

# Color direction

Dome teal metal, aurora mint-violet, mountain umber-blue, star gold.

# Special instructions

All subjects should be reassuring and friendly for ages 3–10. Create one unified scene with visible clues in every quadrant, not a collage or a set of stickers. Prefer rich midtones that separate clearly from warm cream and beige board colors.
