# Scene

A playful rocket sailing past a crescent moon, ringed planet and gentle sparkling stars.

# Composition

Rocket large and central, moon to left, purple ringed planet right, distinct blue and lavender nebula areas.

# Color direction

Midnight-to-medium blue/lavender background, coral and ivory rocket, gold moon. Maintain readable local contrast without scary darkness.

# Special instructions

All subjects should be reassuring and friendly for ages 3–10. Create one unified scene with visible clues in every quadrant, not a collage or a set of stickers.
