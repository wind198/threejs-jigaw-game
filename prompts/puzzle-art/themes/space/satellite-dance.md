# Scene

Two cute satellites orbiting a bright blue Earth with solar panel wings.

# Composition

Earth center disc; satellites left and right; solar panels as clear rectangles; stars corners.

# Color direction

Earth ocean blue and green lands, satellite silver-teal, panel gold, space indigo.

# Special instructions

All subjects should be reassuring and friendly for ages 3–10. Create one unified scene with visible clues in every quadrant, not a collage or a set of stickers. Prefer rich midtones that separate clearly from warm cream and beige board colors.
