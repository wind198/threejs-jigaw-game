# Scene

Two solar-sail ships racing past a bright sun with luminous sail membranes.

# Composition

Sun upper-right glow; two sails as large triangular landmarks; ships small pods; star streaks.

# Color direction

Sails translucent gold and teal, sun warm white-gold, pods coral, space azure.

# Special instructions

All subjects should be reassuring and friendly for ages 3–10. Create one unified scene with visible clues in every quadrant, not a collage or a set of stickers. Prefer rich midtones that separate clearly from warm cream and beige board colors.
