# Scene

A bustling yet friendly orbital city ring with gardens, docks and ships at planetary dawn.

# Composition

City ring arc across mid; garden modules; docked ships; planet horizon below; sun flare upper; many readable landmarks without clutter noise.

# Color direction

City teal-white lights, garden greens, dawn coral atmosphere, space deep blue.

# Special instructions

All subjects should be reassuring and friendly for ages 3–10. Create one unified scene with visible clues in every quadrant, not a collage or a set of stickers. Prefer rich midtones that separate clearly from warm cream and beige board colors.
