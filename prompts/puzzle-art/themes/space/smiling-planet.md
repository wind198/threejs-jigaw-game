# Scene

A big friendly planet with a simple face floating among soft stars and a tiny moon.

# Composition

Planet fills center; moon upper-left; star clusters corners; subtle ring lower arc.

# Color direction

Planet teal and lavender, moon cream-gold, stars soft yellow, deep blue space.

# Special instructions

All subjects should be reassuring and friendly for ages 3–10. Create one unified scene with visible clues in every quadrant, not a collage or a set of stickers. Prefer rich midtones that separate clearly from warm cream and beige board colors.
