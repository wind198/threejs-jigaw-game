# Scene

A lively moonbase plaza with dome stalls, alien-friendly vendors (cute, original) and Earth overhead.

# Composition

Plaza center; dome stalls around; Earth upper disc; rover taxi mid; crater rim background.

# Color direction

Dome cyan glass, stall colors teal coral gold, moon grey-blue, Earth sapphire.

# Special instructions

All subjects should be reassuring and friendly for ages 3–10. Create one unified scene with visible clues in every quadrant, not a collage or a set of stickers. Prefer rich midtones that separate clearly from warm cream and beige board colors.
