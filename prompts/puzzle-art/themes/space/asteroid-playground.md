# Scene

Kids in space suits hopping across friendly rounded asteroids with safety tethers.

# Composition

Three asteroids as stepping stones; kids mid; tethers as clear lines; distant sun.

# Color direction

Asteroid warm grey-lavender, suits bright colors, sun soft gold, space teal-blue.

# Special instructions

All subjects should be reassuring and friendly for ages 3–10. Create one unified scene with visible clues in every quadrant, not a collage or a set of stickers. Prefer rich midtones that separate clearly from warm cream and beige board colors.
