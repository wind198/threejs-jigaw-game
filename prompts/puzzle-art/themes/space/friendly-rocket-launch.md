# Scene

A cute round rocket launching from a colorful pad under a starry but gentle sky.

# Composition

Rocket large center; launch pad lower rectangle; three stars as landmarks; soft planet disc upper-right.

# Color direction

Rocket coral and teal, pad honey, sky indigo (not black void), planet peach.

# Special instructions

All subjects should be reassuring and friendly for ages 3–10. Create one unified scene with visible clues in every quadrant, not a collage or a set of stickers. Prefer rich midtones that separate clearly from warm cream and beige board colors.
