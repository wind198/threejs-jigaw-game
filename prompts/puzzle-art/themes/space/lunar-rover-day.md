# Scene

A friendly wheeled rover exploring grey moon hills with Earth shining above.

# Composition

Rover center; crater left; hill right; Earth disc upper; flag-like colorful marker (no text) mid-right.

# Color direction

Rover orange-teal, moon cool grey, Earth blue-green, sky indigo.

# Special instructions

All subjects should be reassuring and friendly for ages 3–10. Create one unified scene with visible clues in every quadrant, not a collage or a set of stickers. Prefer rich midtones that separate clearly from warm cream and beige board colors.
