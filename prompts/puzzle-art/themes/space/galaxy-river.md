# Scene

A luminous Milky Way-like river of stars flowing between purple nebula banks.

# Composition

Star river S-curve through frame; nebula banks upper and lower; bright core mid; planets as beads along the river.

# Color direction

Star river silver-gold, nebula violet-teal, planet accents coral and lime.

# Special instructions

All subjects should be reassuring and friendly for ages 3–10. Create one unified scene with visible clues in every quadrant, not a collage or a set of stickers. Prefer rich midtones that separate clearly from warm cream and beige board colors.
