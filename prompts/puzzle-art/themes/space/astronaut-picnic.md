# Scene

A round-helmet astronaut sitting on a moon rock sharing cookies with a tiny robot.

# Composition

Astronaut center; robot lower-right; moon rock base; Earth disc upper-left; stars sparse.

# Color direction

Suit white with teal accents, moon grey-blue, Earth sapphire-green, cookie warm gold.

# Special instructions

All subjects should be reassuring and friendly for ages 3–10. Create one unified scene with visible clues in every quadrant, not a collage or a set of stickers. Prefer rich midtones that separate clearly from warm cream and beige board colors.
